Final polish addendum

Direct control: right-click now provides Small, Medium, Large, Close, and Quit. Large preserves the original 440 by 300 maximum footprint; Close hides the avatar while global shortcuts stay armed.

Stable original face: synthetic moving pupils were removed after visual review. The original character artwork remains untouched, and the engine has no pointer-warping capability or authority to move the user's mouse.

Public safety: publication is fail-closed behind an external checksum seal, allowlisted project files, working/staged/full-history scans, local commit and push hooks, exact-remote verification, and fast-forward-only synchronization.

Behavior law: CRIXUS may look alive often. He may move the window rarely. User placement, typing, reading, menus, and mission selection always outrank autonomous behavior.
